Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Tap "Sign in with Apple" on the welcome screen. Revelio uses Apple's secure authentication — no separate password needed. Just use your Apple ID.

Does Revelio work without an account?

You can scan products without signing in, but your scan history won't be saved between sessions. Sign in to keep a permanent history.

Scanning

How do I scan a barcode?

Tap the camera/scanner icon at the bottom of the screen. Point your camera at any food product barcode (UPC or EAN). The app scans automatically — no button press needed.

The app can't find my product. What now?

Some products aren't in the OpenFoodFacts database yet. You can help by adding the product at world.openfoodfacts.org. Once added, Revelio will find it on your next scan.

Why does the app need camera access?

The camera is the core of Revelio — it's how barcodes are read. We only use the camera for scanning; no photos are taken or stored.

Free Tier & Pro Plan

What's included in the free plan?

10 scans per day, resetting at midnight local time. Full ingredient analysis is included on every scan.

What happens when I hit the 10-scan limit?

You'll see a prompt explaining the limit and offering the Pro upgrade. You can dismiss it and come back tomorrow — no charges happen automatically.

What does Pro include?

  • Unlimited scans per day
  • Full scan history with search
  • Priority AI ingredient analysis
  • Additional features as they're released

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Tap the upgrade prompt when it appears, or go to Profile → Upgrade to Pro. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Go to Settings (on your iPhone) → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Revelio. You can cancel anytime — Pro features remain active until the end of the billing period.

Can I restore a purchase on a new device?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Profile → Restore Purchases.

Privacy & Data

What data does Revelio collect?

We collect your Apple ID (for account management), the barcodes you scan, and your scan history. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Is my scan history stored on my device or in the cloud?

Scan history is stored locally on your device by default. We don't sync it to our servers without your explicit consent.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at passalacquatj6302@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account." We'll process the request within 30 days. You can also clear your local scan history at any time via Profile → Settings → Clear Scan History.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Technical Issues

The app crashed. What should I do?

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the issue persists, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version and we'll investigate.

Sign in with Apple isn't working.

Make sure your device has an active internet connection and that you're signed into iCloud (Settings → Apple ID). If the issue continues, try restarting your device.

My scan results look wrong.

Product data comes from OpenFoodFacts, an open community database. Occasionally entries have errors. If you spot one, you can correct it at world.openfoodfacts.org. Tap "Report an issue" on the product screen to flag it directly.
