Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 18, 2026

Revelio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Revelio iOS app, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

Short version: We collect only what's necessary to make the app work. We do not sell your personal data. Scan history is stored on your device. We use a small set of trusted third-party services described below.

1. Information We Collect

Data Type What It Is When Collected
Apple ID (account info) A unique identifier provided by Sign in with Apple. We may receive your name and an optional relay email address if you choose to share them. When you sign in with Apple
Barcode scans The UPC/EAN barcode values you scan. These are used to look up product information. Each time you scan a product
Scan history A log of products you've scanned, including product name, barcode, and date/time of scan. Stored locally on your device after each scan
Subscription status Whether you are on the free tier or Pro plan. Managed by RevenueCat. On app launch and after purchases
Device & usage data Basic crash reports and app performance data (no personally identifiable information). Automatically via system crash reporting

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any data outside of the barcode scanning and account functions described above.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Ingredient analysis: Barcode values are sent to OpenFoodFacts to retrieve product data, and optionally to OpenAI to generate an AI-powered ingredient summary.
  • Scan history: Stored locally on your device so you can review past scans. Not synced to our servers unless cloud sync is explicitly enabled.
  • Account management: Your Apple ID identifier is used to associate your scan history and subscription status with your account.
  • Subscription management: RevenueCat uses your Apple ID and purchase receipts to verify and manage your Pro subscription.
  • App improvement: Crash data helps us identify and fix bugs. No personal data is included in these reports.

3. Third-Party Services

Revelio uses the following trusted third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

Service Purpose Data Shared Privacy Policy
OpenFoodFacts Open-source food product database. Used to retrieve ingredient and nutritional data by barcode. Barcode (UPC/EAN) only openfoodfacts.org/privacy
OpenAI AI-powered ingredient analysis and health summaries. Ingredient list text from the scanned product openai.com/privacy
RevenueCat In-app purchase and subscription management for the Pro plan. Apple ID, purchase receipts, subscription status revenuecat.com/privacy
Twilio Optional SMS/OTP verification (if enabled for account recovery). Phone number (only if you provide it for verification) twilio.com/legal/privacy
Apple (Sign in with Apple) Authentication provider. Apple ID, optional name and relay email apple.com/legal/privacy

We do not sell, rent, or broker your personal information to any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • Scan history is stored locally on your device. It remains there until you delete the app or clear your history in the app settings.
  • Account data (Apple ID identifier, subscription status) is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).
  • Crash/performance data is retained for up to 90 days and is not linked to your identity.

5. Camera Access

Revelio requests access to your device's camera solely to scan barcodes. The camera is active only while the scanner screen is open. We do not capture, store, or transmit photos or video. Camera data is processed entirely on-device in real time and is never sent to our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Revelio is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of AI ingredient analysis (scans will still use OpenFoodFacts data)
  • Export your scan history from within the app

To delete your account or request a copy of your data, email us at passalacquatj6302@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request — Revelio". We will respond within 30 days.

You can also delete your scan history at any time directly within the app via Profile → Settings → Clear Scan History.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including using HTTPS for all network communications and relying on Apple's secure enclave for authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you via the app or by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Revelio after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
